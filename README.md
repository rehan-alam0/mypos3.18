**MyPOS v3.18.0**

MyPOS is an offline point-of-sale and shop management program for Windows. It works without internet, and all data stays on your own computer.

**What's new**

**Easy Mode.** A theme with a larger menu, bigger buttons and clearer text. Choose it in Settings → Themes.

**Sales screen.** Walk-in and New customer buttons. New customers can be added from a quick popup. The search dropdown layout is the default, and the cart is wider.

**Discounts.** A percentage or rupee discount on the whole bill, flowing into invoices, sales, profit and balances.

**Purchases.** Credit (udhaar) purchases, purchase returns, and supplier advances shown in green, with cash received back recorded.

**Employees.** Staff records, advances, and monthly salaries. Salaries count as expenses, and advances are deducted.

**WhatsApp drafts.** Choose customers and a message. Drafts open in WhatsApp for you to send. Nothing is sent automatically.

**Ledgers.** A detailed view lists items under each bill, and it prints. Debit and credit memos with reasons.

**Dashboard.** Credit given, recovered, outstanding, and a six-month recovery chart.

**Roles and access.** The owner chooses what each role can use. Deleting customers, suppliers and sales is owner-only unless granted.

**Keyboard shortcuts.** Custom keys for each action. F1, F2, F8 and F9 still work.

**Menu and top bar.** Regrouped sidebar, Receive Payment in Quick Access, and clear icons in every theme.

**Login.** New design with Remember Me and a status bar.

**Please note**
- Per-product discounts were removed. Discounts apply to the whole bill.
- Deleting customers, suppliers and sales needs the owner's permission.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or later.
